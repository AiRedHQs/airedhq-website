# Company Research: Education, Career and Immersive-Technology Providers

**Research date:** 29 July 2026  
**Companies reviewed:** ThinkLABS, Next Education, Veative, Umety, LaunchMyCareer, EmsigPro, STEMpedia, Nextechoasis and Sensible-VR.

> **Reach note:** Reach figures below are publicly reported by the companies or their partners and have not been independently audited. Where a current user, school or country total was not available, the stated reach describes the company’s disclosed market or geographic focus. Historical figures are labelled as such.

---

## 1. ThinkLABS

**One-line info:** A historical Indian educational-robotics company that brought hands-on robotics, programming and embedded-systems learning to schools and colleges.

**Reach:** Historically, its i-Pitara robotics initiative was reported to have reached **1 million+ children across 2,000+ schools**. The original business and its RoboLAB assets later changed ownership, so a current independent ThinkLABS deployment figure is not publicly available.

**Product offering:**
- i-Pitara and i-Pitara Pro robotics and electronics kits.
- CiMPLE/THiNK visual programming tools for controlling robots.
- RoboLAB / Robotic Learning System for converting school computer labs into robotics labs.
- Curriculum-mapped learning material, teacher training, student workshops and robotics competitions.

**Category:** Educational Robotics / STEM EdTech / Hands-on Science Education

**Small description:** ThinkLABS Technosolutions was an IIT Bombay-linked venture focused on experiential science and technology education. Its offering combined physical building components, sensors and controllers with visual programming software and structured classroom content. It is best treated as a historical brand rather than a clearly operating standalone company today: public sources report the acquisition of ThinkLABS and/or its robotics business by other education providers.

**Sources:** [EducationWorld – ThinkLABS RoboLAB](https://educationworld.in/thinklabs-robolab/) · [Next Education – company history](https://nexteducation.in/about.html) · [Historical reach reported by a former core-team member](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/anandramaswami_back-in-2008-at-an-iit-bombayincubated-activity-7380252866524930048-LJiL) · [Business Standard – FitKids acquisition](https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/fitkids-acquires-thinklabs-adds-100-more-schools-to-portfolio-116100500952_1.html)

## 2. Next Education

**One-line info:** An Indian B2B K–12 EdTech company providing schools with curriculum, digital classrooms, school-management platforms, labs and academic partnership solutions.

**Reach:** The company reports **12 million+ students, 18,000+ schools and 240,000+ teachers**, representing about 10% of India’s private-school-going children. It primarily serves India and also lists a Middle East office in Dubai.

**Product offering:**
- Curriculum solutions, books and learning content, including NextBooks and NextCurriculum.
- TeachNext digital/smart-classroom solutions and multimedia content.
- NextLab and hands-on laboratory or activity-based learning solutions.
- LearnNext and other self-learning products.
- School ERP, LMS, CRM, communication and administration platforms.
- End-to-end academic and operational partnership programmes for schools.

**Category:** K–12 B2B EdTech / School SaaS / Digital Curriculum and Classroom Solutions

**Small description:** Founded in 2007, Next Education positions itself as an end-to-end technology and academic partner for K–12 institutions. Its portfolio spans classroom instruction, curriculum, self-learning, school administration and institutional support, allowing schools to buy individual products or a more integrated transformation programme.

**Sources:** [Next Education – official website](https://nexteducation.in/) · [Next Education – about and company history](https://nexteducation.in/about.html)

## 3. Veative

**One-line info:** An immersive-learning company that develops curriculum-aligned VR/WebXR educational content, simulations and analytics for schools, higher education and training.

**Reach:** Public industry reports describe Veative solutions as being used in **25+ countries**; other company-related communications have cited more than 20 countries. A current school or learner total is not clearly disclosed.

**Product offering:**
- Interactive, curriculum-aligned K–12 VR modules, especially for STEM subjects.
- Browser-accessible WebVR/WebXR learning experiences.
- Learning-management, assessment, scoring and student-progress analytics.
- English-language learning with immersive scenarios and speech-based interaction.
- Career exploration, higher-education and enterprise training simulations.
- VR classroom content packages and integrations with education-focused headsets.

**Category:** Immersive Learning / Virtual Reality EdTech / XR Content Platform

**Small description:** Veative was established as an education-first VR content provider, using immersive visualization to make abstract concepts easier to understand. Its model combines instructional content with assessments and teacher analytics rather than offering VR as entertainment alone. Current Umety and Lenovo materials show significant overlap with Veative-branded content, but the exact present-day corporate or brand relationship is not explained clearly in a single public source.

**Sources:** [UNICEF Innovation – Veative Labs](https://www.unicef.org/innovation/stories/veative-labs-making-educational-content-more-accessible-through-webvr) · [VR/AR Association – Veative coverage](https://www.thevrara.com/news2/tag/Veative%2BLabs) · [Lenovo Support – Veative content](https://support.lenovo.com/ca/en/solutions/ht515965-veative-immersive-and-interactive-content-for-virtual-reality-vr-devices) · [Umety/Lenovo support page showing the newer branding](https://support.lenovo.com/my/en/solutions/ht509873-lenovo-vr-classroom-2-veative-limited-for-lenovo)

## 4. Umety

**One-line info:** A K–12 immersive-learning platform combining AR/VR educational content, education-first headsets, classroom management and learning analytics.

**Reach:** Umety presents itself as a global platform headquartered in San Francisco with operational links to India, but it does not publish a reliable current total for schools, countries or learners. Its **content reach** includes **1,000+ immersive lessons and virtual experiences, 1,500+ interactive 3D models and 300,000 explorable 360° locations**.

**Product offering:**
- Immersive lessons in STEM, social sciences, English-language learning, virtual tours and career exploration.
- UmetyVR education-first headset with preloaded content and offline operation.
- Cross-platform access through VR/AR headsets, interactive flat panels, Chromebooks and tablets.
- Classroom orchestration tools for launching, pausing and managing lessons across student devices.
- Student assessments, reports, engagement tracking and learning analytics.
- Content and experiences designed for special education and inclusive learning.

**Category:** Immersive Learning / K–12 XR EdTech / VR Hardware and SaaS Platform

**Small description:** Umety provides a complete immersive-classroom stack rather than only a library of 3D content. Schools can use its content on existing screens and devices or deploy Umety’s offline-capable headset, while teachers and administrators manage classes, profiles, assessments and reports through the platform.

**Sources:** [Umety – official website](https://www.umety.com/) · [Umety – about](https://www.umety.com/about/) · [Umety – LinkedIn company profile](https://www.linkedin.com/company/umety)

## 5. LaunchMyCareer

**One-line info:** A career-guidance and career-readiness platform helping young people discover interests, explore occupations, receive counselling and develop workplace skills.

**Reach:** A publicly announced partnership aimed to make the platform available through **6,000+ CSC Academies across India** for students from Class 8 onward. A current active-user or school total is not publicly disclosed.

**Product offering:**
- Interest, aptitude, personality and self-discovery assessments.
- Live or virtual career counselling with educators and career experts.
- Career libraries, personalised roadmaps and occupation matching.
- Immersive 360°/VR career exploration and virtual workplace experiences.
- Workplace-readiness and future-skills development.
- Parent, educator and counsellor involvement in the student’s career journey.

**Category:** Career Guidance EdTech / Career Discovery / Student Employability and Skills

**Small description:** LaunchMyCareer is designed as a journey from self-discovery to informed career choice rather than a single psychometric test. Its platform uses gamification, counselling, immersive career experiences and skills content to help secondary-school learners connect their interests and abilities with possible education and employment pathways.

**Sources:** [Tata Tele Business Services – LaunchMyCareer case study](https://www.tatatelebusiness.com/case-studies/launchmycareer-platform-enriches-its-virtual-career-counselling-sessions-with-ttbs-enabled-zoom/) · [LaunchMyCareer/LMC – LinkedIn company profile](https://in.linkedin.com/company/lmcgroupworld) · [CSC Academy partnership announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/launchmycareer_launchmycareer-cscacademy-career-activity-6881220755514761216-7zae) · [Exchange4media – platform description](https://www.exchange4media.com/pr-and-corporate-communication-news/launchmycareer-appoints-hillknowlton-strategies-as-communications-partner-118218.html)

## 6. EmsigPro

**One-line info:** A Hyderabad-based overseas-education consultancy focused on helping Indian students pursue engineering, medical and allied higher education in Germany.

**Reach:** Primarily serves students in India seeking education in Germany, with a local presence reported in Kukatpally, Hyderabad. No verified current student, university-partner or country-volume figure is publicly available.

**Product offering:**
- University and programme-selection counselling for Germany.
- Application and admissions support.
- Visa guidance and documentation assistance.
- German-language classes and cultural/integration preparation.
- Preparatory pathways and guidance for engineering, medicine and related disciplines.

**Category:** Study-Abroad Consultancy / International Higher-Education Services / Germany Education Specialist

**Small description:** EmsigPro acts as an advisory and application-support service for students navigating the German higher-education system. Its public profiles emphasize end-to-end counselling, admissions and visa assistance, with additional language and preparatory support. Public information is limited, and its official website was not consistently accessible during research.

**Sources:** [EmsigPro – LinkedIn profile](https://in.linkedin.com/school/emsigpro/) · [Collegedunia – EmsigPro overview](https://collegedunia.com/institute/3930-emsigpro-the-german-company-hyderabad) · [EmsigPro – Instagram profile](https://www.instagram.com/emsigpro/)

## 7. STEMpedia

**One-line info:** An Indian K–12 STEM EdTech company offering coding and AI software, educational robots, school innovation labs, curriculum and teacher training.

**Reach:** STEMpedia reports **300,000+ monthly active users** for PictoBlox. Its Codeavour innovation competition is reported to have impacted **300,000 students across 60+ countries**.

**Product offering:**
- PictoBlox block-coding and Python platform covering coding, AI, machine learning, robotics and XR/VR.
- Quarky programmable AI and robotics kits and add-ons.
- Wizbot educational robots for foundational numeracy and computational thinking.
- evive STEM kit and Dabble mobile application.
- AI & Robotics Labs, STEM Innovation Labs and Atal Tinkering Lab solutions.
- CBSE- and ICSE-aligned coding, AI and robotics curricula.
- Teacher-training programmes, projects, courses and the Codeavour competition.

**Category:** STEM Education / Coding and AI EdTech / Educational Robotics

**Small description:** Founded in 2017 by IIT Kanpur alumni, STEMpedia combines software, programmable hardware and school programmes into a one-stop STEM-learning ecosystem. Its products support beginner block coding through more advanced Python, AI, robotics and maker projects, serving individual learners as well as complete school-lab deployments.

**Sources:** [STEMpedia – official website](https://thestempedia.com/) · [STEMpedia – LinkedIn company profile](https://in.linkedin.com/company/agiloresearch) · [STEMpedia product/documentation portal](https://ai.thestempedia.com/) · [Google Play – STEMpedia developer profile](https://play.google.com/store/apps/dev?id=8270219097576751916)

## 8. Nextechoasis

**One-line info:** An India-based AR/VR development company creating custom immersive experiences for education, training, real estate, agriculture and healthcare.

**Reach:** The company lists an office in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh and markets services across several industries. Its website does not disclose a meaningful number of clients, deployments, students or countries served.

**Product offering:**
- VR classrooms and interactive 3D STEM-learning experiences.
- Virtual real-estate walkthroughs and design visualization.
- VR education and simulations for modern agricultural techniques.
- Healthcare equipment, treatment and patient-safety training simulations.
- Custom AR, VR and mixed-reality applications, 3D content and enterprise experiences.

**Category:** XR Development Services / Immersive Training / AR-VR Solutions

**Small description:** Nextechoasis is primarily a custom-development and services company rather than a single standardized education product. It applies extended reality to visualize complex ideas, simulate high-cost or risky tasks and create interactive customer or learner experiences across multiple sectors.

## 9. Sensible-VR

**One-line info:** A Japan-based education-technology company building practical, privacy-conscious and offline-first VR systems for classroom learning.

**Reach:** Based in Higashiomi, Shiga, Japan, and founded in 2024, Sensible-VR targets schools internationally. It does not yet publish a consolidated total for schools, learners or countries served.

**Product offering:**
- Education-focused VR hardware selected for durability, low maintenance and classroom use.
- Preconfigured, localized educational-content libraries.
- Offline-first deployment that can operate without continuous broadband or cloud dependence.
- English-language learning with **78 lessons**, four levels and offline speech recognition.
- Teacher-friendly implementation, privacy-by-design and support for focused or special-needs learning environments.

**Category:** Education VR / Offline Immersive Learning / Classroom Hardware and Content Platform

**Small description:** Sensible-VR differentiates itself from consumer gaming headsets by optimizing the complete system for real schools. Its emphasis is on affordability, low technical overhead, learner privacy, distraction-free use and reliable operation in locations with weak connectivity.

**Sources:** [Sensible-VR – official website](https://sensible-vr.com/) · [Sensible-VR – LinkedIn company profile](https://jp.linkedin.com/company/sensible-vr) · [Sensible-VR – English-language learning details](https://sensible-vr.com/)
